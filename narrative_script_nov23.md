# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment: Miami Art Week Opportunities

**Casual Coffee Chat Style - Encouraging & Uplifting**

Hey friend, pull up a chair. Miami Art Week is right around the corner—November 30 to December 6, 2026—and the energy is already buzzing. If you’ve been dreaming of getting your digital or physical work in front of the global crowd that descends on Miami for Art Basel and the satellites, I’ve got some real ones for you. No fluff, just clear paths that still feel open or just closed but worth knowing for next time.

First up, the one that felt pure Web3 magic: Render Network’s Miami After Dark. They were selecting 10-15 artists for a massive 50-foot projection on Miami Beach on December 2. Theme was Miami After Dark, pieces up to 2 minutes, must be rendered on their network (they covered the credits). No application fee at all. Deadline was late October/early November. It’s closed now, but the fact they ran a free, tech-forward open call during the biggest week shows the space is real. Google “Render Network Miami After Dark” or check rendernetwork.com and their Typeform for future years. They wanted original digital work, any nationality in some listings, US-focused in others. Simple submission—resume, cover letter vibes, the piece itself.

Next, One Love Art DAO and guest curator Fidel Amos (BrunchByFidel) ran a super accessible open call for their FRONTERA exhibition at the YVE Hotel gallery during Miami Art Week. Selected artists get exhibited in the physical/digital gallery space at Hotel Yve. It was free—literally just follow the tagged accounts, share ONE piece of art on X, and tag a friend. They kept saying “yes pls submit” even after the formal form deadline. Still popping as of mid-November. This is pure Web3 energy: DAO, community, no gatekeeping. Search “One Love Art DAO FRONTERA” or hit @OneLoveArtDAO and @FidelEverywhere on X. They’re looking for work that challenges understanding and shifts perspectives. Super low barrier—basically one image and engagement. Perfect for digital artists.

Then there’s Downtown Miami Art Week, the fourth edition across three galleries on East Flagler (Aura Copeland Gallery, Les Couleurs, Aura Copeland Contemporary). December 1-6, with VIP preview Nov 30. Emerging and established artists welcome. Registration via their site lescouleursmiamibeach.com/artweek—they had spots left recently and extended delivery. It’s a real physical exhibition in the heart of Downtown. There is a wall-space reservation fee (check Moonclerk link), so only if it fits your budget, but the exposure during Art Week is solid. Email work to Lcmiamiarts@outlook.com or contact curator Gabrielly Paz. Google “Downtown Miami Art Week 2026” or “Les Couleurs Miami Art Week”.

Art Miami Magazine had a free-to-apply open call for their Special Print Edition distributed during Art Week. Deadline was October 20. They want bio, statement, Instagram/website. Selected artists get editorial features. Application itself is free; later participation might involve costs for the print pages. Still, a clear path to print visibility. Form is on their site, or email them. Search “Art Miami Magazine open call Miami Art Week 2026”.

Americans for Immigrant Justice had a free digital exhibition “Love and Belonging: Immigrant Stories” on displays at Little Haiti Cultural Complex during Art Week. Deadline extended to Nov 21. Unpaid but real digital showing. Theme around belonging and love. Google that or check their Instagram/Facebook.

For the database, these are the still-relevant or recently active ones. Deadlines move fast, so always double-check the URL. We’re building a searchable list so you can type MIAMI and see what’s open.

You’ve got this. Your work belongs in the room. Go apply where it still fits, or bookmark for next year. Miami rewards the ones who show up ready.

Coffee’s on me next time we check the list.
