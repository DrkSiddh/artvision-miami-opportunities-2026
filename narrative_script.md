# ArtVision Podcast Special Segment: Miami Art Week Free Exhibition Opportunities

**Style**: Conversational, like a friend over coffee at the café — casual but authoritative. Encouraging, uplifting, never punching down.

---

Hey, grab that latte and lean in. I just spent the morning digging through the wild corners of the internet — X, Instagram, artist forums, all of it — because Miami Art Week is right around the corner (November 30 through December 6, 2026) and there are still some genuinely free ways for artists to get their work up and seen while the whole art world is in town.

No gatekeeping, no “you should already know this.” Just the real ones that are still open, free to apply, and actually put the work on view (and often get you into the event too).

First up, and this one is pure Web3 energy: **One Love Art DAO** has their FRONTERA open call. They’re looking for works that challenge our understanding and shift perspectives. Selected artists will be exhibited in their digital art gallery right at the Hotel Yve during Miami Art Week. Deadline is tomorrow — November 15, 2026. Completely free. You drop into their Google form, share your piece (or pieces), a short bio, and whatever statement makes the work sing. It’s a short form, probably half a dozen fields max, and the main “receipt” they want is the art itself plus your info. Link is right here: https://forms.gle/DjERA61QPdZEf19SA or just Google “OneLoveArtDAO FRONTERA Miami.”

Same circle has a super low-lift flash call with Fidel Amos (@FidelEverywhere) and BrunchByFidel. They’re exhibiting 25 artists at the same Hotel Yve. To be considered you just follow the tagged profiles, share ONE piece of art on X, and tag one friend. Deadline is basically this weekend — they said aim for the end of the weekend. Zero formal questions, one art receipt. Perfect if you already have something ready to post.

Next, digital artists, this is the big-scale one: **Render Network — Miami After Dark**. They’re selecting 10–15 artists for a 50-foot large-scale projection on Miami Beach (at The National Hotel) on December 2, 2026. Theme is Miami After Dark, pieces up to about two minutes, and they must be rendered on the Render Network — but they cover the rendering credits for selected artists. No application fee. Deadline also November 15, 2026. The Typeform takes about seven minutes: basic artist info, the work details, a bio or statement, and a link to the rendered piece. So questions are light, main receipt is that one (or a few) rendered works plus your story. Form: https://renderfoundation.typeform.com/to/NGgLRjhY — or search “Render Network Miami After Dark Typeform.”

These are the ones that are still truly free, clear on the deadline, and put the art on display during the week. Everything else either closed, charged a fee, or was pay-to-play wall space.

You’ve got this. Drop the applications today if either of these fits, and I’ll keep updating the database so we can surface fresh ones every day. Search “MIAMI” plus the keyword and the URL will pop right up while they’re still accepting.

That’s the coffee-table download. Go make something beautiful and get it in front of the people who are already flying in.
